# CritLine Forever

Critline remembers your highest normal and critical hits and heals, and shows a message on screen when you break a record.

**This is an unofficial port of Critline to WoW: Forever, published with the author's permission.**
Critline was written by **Lombra**, who owns the copyright ([original project](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/critline)). It was originally created by Sordit. The port is by The_Yonaz and is modified and distributed with **Lombra's written permission**. Please report problems with this version here, not to Lombra.

WoW: Forever doesn't give addons access to the combat log, so this port reads your target's combat events instead. Records are kept per damage school (Physical, Fire, Frost, …), and by default only while you're playing solo. Optional per-spell records guess which spell hit from the spell you just cast.

Commands: `/cl` opens the options, and `/cl debug` shows why a hit was or wasn't recorded.
